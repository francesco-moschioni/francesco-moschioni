I am a Pre-Doctoral Research Assistant at Universitat Pompeu Fabra, where I work with Jan Eeckhout on an ERC project on labour markets and market power. I hold an MSc in Economics and Social Sciences from Bocconi University.

My interests are in urban and spatial economics, industrial organization and labour economics. I study how prices, geography and access to alternatives shape choices in urban markets, using structural demand models and large administrative data.

Work in progress

Optimal Urban Transportation Policy under Congestion Pricing: Evidence from New York City

Working title

Abstract

What combination of a cordon toll, transit fares and frequencies, and taxi and ride-hail surcharges maximizes welfare in New York City, and how far is the 2025 Congestion Relief Zone from that optimum? I estimate a demand system for eleven modes on 2025 trip data, select the specification with a pre-registered tournament scored on the observed shock of the toll's launch, and embed it in a general-equilibrium model of road congestion and transit service.

Bridge and Tunnel: The Geography of Substitution under New York's Congestion Pricing

MSc thesis, Bocconi University, 2026. Supervisor: Francesco Decarolis

Abstract

I estimate a multimodal random-coefficients nested-logit demand model for New York City's transport market, covering eight modes and 263 taxi zones, and evaluate the no-toll regime as a structural counterfactual. Income alone does not order the demand-side incidence of the charge: spatial differences in access to transport alternatives largely determine who can adjust.
