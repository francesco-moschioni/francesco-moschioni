About

Francesco Moschioni

MSc Student in Economics · Bocconi University · Dondena Centre

I study applied microeconomics at Bocconi, with a focus on markets where space matters — particularly transport, urban economics, and industrial organization. My work combines causal inference (DiD, event studies) with structural demand estimation, using large datasets in Python and Stata. I am currently a Research Assistant at the Social Inclusion Lab (Dondena Centre), working on social dispersion and educational inclusion.

Research & Projects
Competition & Spatial Dynamics in Transport
MSc thesis in empirical industrial organization. Studies how competition and spatial structure shape demand in urban transport, integrating mobility, weather, and demographic data (MTA, TLC, Citi Bike). Builds modular Python pipelines with structural demand estimation moving from logit toward random coefficient models.
